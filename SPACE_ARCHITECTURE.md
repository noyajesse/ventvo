# Ventvo Space Architecture

## Purpose

This document clarifies the Space concept established by Ventvo's existing Vent foundation.

A Space is not a subreddit, group, channel, forum, topic board, or traditional social-media community.

A Space exists to organize **how Voices meet expression**.

The original foundation remains important: a Vent creates a Space that communicates how the creator wants that expression to be experienced. The clarification is that the Space can become a shared experience rather than remaining limited to the first Vent.

## Core Relationship

```text
Voice
  ↓
Vent
  ↓
Space
  ↓
Voices meet expressions
  ↓
Space activity
  ↓
Moments
  ↓
Feed
  ↓
tap Moment
  ↓
its Space
```

A Space begins from an intentional Vent. Other Voices may enter and contribute expressions when those expressions genuinely belong within the experience of that Space.

The first Vent establishes the initial context. Participation does not mean joining a conventional community; it means entering an experience and meeting what is happening there.

## Two Space Modes

Ventvo currently has only two Space modes.

### Just Listen

**Need:** "I want to be heard."

The creator is asking for presence rather than advice, solutions, or discussion.

```text
Expression
  ↓
Presence
  ↓
Heard
```

Just Listen protects the creator's intention. Normal comments, advice, Echo Notes, and open discussion are not part of this experience.

The meaningful outcome is:

> Someone heard me.

### Discussion

**Need:** "I want to explore this with other Voices."

The creator welcomes perspectives and conversation around the expression.

```text
Expression
  ↓
Perspective
  ↓
Echo / Echo Note
  ↓
Understanding
```

Discussion should not become a conventional comment thread. Responses should preserve Voice identity and contribute perspective rather than optimize for engagement.

## Participation

A Space may contain expressions from multiple Voices.

A later Voice can participate when their expression genuinely belongs within the Space's experience. Ventvo should not automatically force an expression into a Space based only on topic similarity.

Future Resonance or AI-assisted discovery may suggest that experiences are related, but suggestions must not remove the Voice's control over where they participate.

## Space Activity

Space activity is the meaningful activity created when Voices express and meet one another inside a Space.

The activity is not defined as a chronological list of posts. The Space should feel like an environment with context, presence, expression, connection, and understanding.

## Moments and Feed

A Moment is a meaningful glimpse of Space activity.

The Feed is a discovery layer for Moments occurring within Spaces. It is not a separate social-media destination that replaces the Space.

When a Voice taps a Moment, the intended destination is the Space from which that Moment came. The Voice should be able to experience the surrounding context rather than being sent to a generic post/comment page.

```text
Space
  ↓
Space activity
  ↓
Moment
  ↓
Feed
  ↓
tap Moment
  ↓
return to its Space
```

## Product Guardrails

Space must not gradually become:

- Subreddits
- Facebook-style groups
- Discord-style channels
- Follower-based communities
- Popularity feeds
- Generic topic forums

The defining question is:

> **How should people meet this expression?**

not:

> **How can we organize more content?**

Every Space feature should strengthen Ventvo's principles:

- Expression over performance
- Understanding over popularity
- Connection over engagement
- Voice over traditional profiles
- Moments over generic posts
- Meaningful interaction over reaction farming

## Current Implementation Boundary

This document describes the intended product architecture. It does **not** claim that shared Spaces, persistent multi-Voice participation, Moments, or Feed-to-Space navigation are already implemented.

The existing prototype remains the implementation baseline until these systems are deliberately built and validated.

Future implementation should preserve the existing Just Listen and Open Discussion semantics rather than replacing them with generic social-media interactions.
