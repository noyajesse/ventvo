# Ventvo Maps — Product Specification

## Definition
A **Map is a landscape of connected experiences.** It is Ventvo's highest-level experience-discovery layer containing Moments, Echo ripples, Resonance evidence, Threads, clusters/areas, emerging patterns, and neighboring experiences.

> **A Map is a landscape, not a category.**

## Core hierarchy
```
Moment → Echo → Resonance → Thread → Map
```
This is conceptual and emergent, not a mandatory progression.

## Formation
Maps are discovered rather than manually created. They emerge when enough meaningful Threads and connected experiences form a recognizable landscape. Existing formation thresholds, distinct Voice requirements, privacy, and safety rules remain authoritative.

## Hybrid Map
Every Map has two views of the same state:

**✦ Constellation — feel the landscape**  
**≡ Landscape — understand the landscape**

Switching views preserves the same selected Moment/Thread and Map state.

## Constellation
The Constellation must feel like Ventvo, not a scientific graph or data visualization. Visual metaphor: **night sky + water ripples + distant lights + organic paths**.

Use soft lights for experiences, organic paths for Threads, atmospheric clusters for areas, ripples for Echo activity, subtle responses for Resonance, and empty space where evidence is sparse. Avoid rigid circles/lines and technical node/edge language.

## Wandering
Users move through the landscape, gently zoom, tap experiences, follow paths, enter clusters, open Threads/Moments, and return to the wider landscape. The Map must not become an infinite feed.

## Selecting an experience
The selected experience becomes the visual anchor. Allow **Open Moment**, **Resonate**, **Explore Echo ripple**, and **Continue wandering**. Nearby related experiences may subtly respond.

## Selecting a Thread
Focus the landscape around the Thread, e.g. **Not Being Understood — 8 connected experiences**. Switching to Landscape keeps the same Thread selected.

## Clusters
Clusters are atmospheric areas where related experiences gather, not hard category bubbles.

## Landscape View
Landscape View is the grounded readable interpretation of the same Map. Example:

# Feeling Alone
> A landscape of experiences around feeling unseen and disconnected.

Then Threads such as **Not Being Understood**, **Pulling Away**, and **Wanting Someone to Notice**, each with a short evidence-grounded explanation and access to its Moments.

## Emerging areas
When a pattern is not mature:
> **Something is forming**
>
> A few experiences are beginning to connect around wanting someone to notice.

Do not manufacture certainty.

## Evolution
Maps may gain Moments/Echoes/Threads, develop new areas, merge or split Threads, become quiet, and connect to neighboring Maps.

## Privacy and safety
Private Crash Outs and private Moments must never silently become Map points. Resonance, Thread, and Map formation remain subject to privacy/safety policy.

## Animation
Keep movement calm: new experience gently appears, Echo softly ripples, Resonance subtly pulses, Thread paths gradually appear, emerging areas gently gather, and selection causes nearby experiences to respond. No flashy celebrations or gamification.

## What Maps are not
Maps are not social graphs, relationship graphs, popularity dashboards, emotional analytics, personality maps, diagnostic maps, follower networks, or engagement dashboards.

## Emotional journey
> “There are other experiences around mine.”
>
> “These are different but connected.”
>
> “This one feels familiar.”
>
> “I wonder where this path goes.”

## Principle
> **A Map should make the invisible shape of shared experience feel discoverable.**

## Final model
**Moment** — individual experience  
**Echo** — personal ripple  
**Resonance** — recognition  
**Thread** — connected story  
**Map** — larger landscape

**Constellation + Landscape = the same Map.**
