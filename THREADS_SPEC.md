# Ventvo Threads — Product Specification

## Definition
A **Thread** is a recognizable path through multiple experiences. It is not a group chat, comment chain, community, or manually managed collection.

> **A Thread is a story without a storyteller.**

## Purpose
Threads answer: **“What story is forming between these experiences?”**

Example: “Nobody really knows me.” → “I stopped explaining myself.” → “I don't know how to let people in.”

## Formation
Threads are discovered, not normally created manually. Existing Resonance formation policies remain authoritative: meaningful connection types, sufficient strength/evidence, accepted/active lifecycle evidence, distinct Voice identities, privacy/safety eligibility, and existing thresholds. A single Resonance cannot manufacture a Thread.

## Structure
```
Thread → Experiences → Moments → Echo ripples
```

Echoes remain attached to their source Moment; Threads do not become Echo dumps.

## Naming
Names describe shared experiences: **Not Being Understood**, **Pulling Away**, **Wanting Someone to Notice**, **Feeling Out of Place**, **Starting Over**. Avoid people/category labels such as “Lonely People.” Descriptions must be grounded in actual evidence and never diagnose users.

## Visual model
In Constellation, Threads are organic paths: curved, soft, imperfect, fading or overlapping. In Landscape View, Threads become readable stories through connected Moments.

## Evolution
Threads may gain experiences, branch, overlap, split, merge, become quiet, or become dormant. Users do not manually maintain this structure.

## Emerging Threads
When evidence is still developing:
> **Something is emerging**
>
> A few experiences are beginning to connect around a similar feeling.

Do not present uncertain patterns as established truth.

## Actions
Keep actions minimal: **Explore Thread**, **Explore related experiences**, **Open Map**. Do not add Join Thread, Follow Thread, Like Thread, or Subscribe.

## Privacy and safety
Private Moments, Crash Outs, relationships, and conversations cannot become public Thread material without explicit visibility permission. Existing privacy/safety vetoes remain authoritative.

## AI boundary
AI may eventually assist with grounded Thread naming, summaries, overlap detection, and emerging-pattern suggestions. It must not invent diagnoses or unsupported emotional interpretations.

## Principle
> **Threads reveal stories that already exist between experiences.**
