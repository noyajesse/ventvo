# Ventvo Principles

## 1. Voice Over Profile

Ventvo is built around expression, not popularity.

A person's identity is not defined by:
- followers
- likes
- status

A Voice is understood through the moments they choose to share.

---

## 2. Connection Over Engagement

Ventvo does not optimize for endless scrolling or reactions.

The goal is not:
- more likes
- more comments
- more attention

The goal is:
- meaningful resonance
- feeling understood
- discovering shared experiences

---

## 3. Moments, Not Posts

A Vent is not just content.

A Vent is a released moment from someone's Voice.

Every moment should represent:
- a thought
- a feeling
- an experience
- a situation

---

## 4. Boundaries Matter

The person creating a Vent chooses how others interact with it.

Interaction spaces:

### Just Listen
Purpose:
A person wants to be heard.

Allows:
- Resonance

Does not allow:
- Discussion
- Echo Notes
- Advice

---

### Open Discussion
Purpose:
A person wants different perspectives.

Allows:
- Resonance
- Echo Notes
- Discussion
- Crash Out (if enabled)

---

## 5. Resonance Over Popularity

Ventvo measures connection, not popularity.

We avoid:
- follower counts
- public popularity rankings
- engagement pressure

A small meaningful connection is more valuable than a large audience.

---

## 6. Echo Notes Are Reflections

Echo Notes are not comments.

They are personal reflections from another Voice that connected with a moment.

A good Echo Note says:

"I understand."
"I felt something similar."
"This connected with me."

It is not:
- advice
- criticism
- judgment

---

## 7. Temporary Moments Can Matter

Crash Out exists because intense emotions are often temporary.

Ventvo creates temporary spaces where people can connect during those moments without creating permanent pressure.

---

## 8. Every Feature Must Solve a Human Problem

A feature should only exist if it helps people:

- express themselves
- feel understood
- find meaningful connection

If a feature only looks interesting but does not solve a real problem, it does not belong in Ventvo.
